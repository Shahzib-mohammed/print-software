6. Payment Settlement Flow (Behind the Scenes)
Customer pays via gateway checkout

Gateway processes UPI/card payment

Gateway splits payout: shop's share -> shop's linked bank account

Platform's share, if any -> platform's account, per commission model

Webhook confirms payment to PrintQueue backend

Order status updated to PAID, job released to printer
